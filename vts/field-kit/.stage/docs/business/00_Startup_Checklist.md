# Updated Client-Ready Plan

## 1. Service Packaging & Pricing

**Goal:** Turn your two service ideas into clear, sellable offers with defined scope and pricing.

**Key Actions:**
- Finalize service names and positioning
  - Network & IoT Support (3 tiers) 
  - IT & Desktop Support (3 tiers)
- Write clear, benefit-focused descriptions (client language, 2 to 3 sentences long)
- Define exact scope for each starter service (what’s included vs. extra)
  - What’s included in each tier
  - Custom work is an option, price determined per request
- Set concrete pricing (hourly + flat-rate options)
- Create simple service tiers if useful (e.g., Basic Optimization vs. Optimization + Remote Support Setup), include whats NOT included only if there needs to be a clear and explicit statement made, based upon industry norms

**Deliverables:**
- One clean document describing each service with pricing and scope
- Clear boundaries so you can say “that’s outside of scope” confidently

## 2. Delivery & Operations Basics

**Goal:** Build the minimum systems needed to take on a client without looking disorganized.

**Key Actions:**
- Create a simple intake / discovery process (questions to ask before quoting)
- Draft a one-page Service Agreement + Remote Access Consent form
- Build a basic job checklist / documentation template
- Set up simple invoicing (Square, Wave, or PayPal to start)
- Define your follow-up process after a job (thank you + testimonial request)

**Deliverables:**
- Intake form (Google Form or printable)
- Service Agreement template
- Job documentation checklist
- Invoicing method chosen and tested

## 3. Portfolio & Proof

**Goal:** Have real examples and proof you can show (even before you have client work).

**Key Actions:**
- Document work you’ve already done on your own property (Starlink + Deco optimization, network segregation, ad-blocking, etc.)
- Take before/after photos
- Write 1–2 short case study style summaries (problem → what you did → results)
- Prepare a simple way to show this (folder, Obsidian notes, or simple PDF)

**Deliverables:**
- 1–2 documented examples from your own setup
- Before/after photos
- Short write-ups you can eventually use on your website or share with prospects

## 4. Online Presence

**Goal:** Be findable and look credible online.

**Key Actions:**
- Build a basic landing page (Hugo on your homelab or faster tool)
- Create a Facebook Business Page
- Use the service descriptions and pricing from Step 1
- Add your portfolio examples from Step 3

**Deliverables:**
- Live landing page
- Active Facebook Business Page with service information

## 5. Lead Generation

**Goal:** Start getting conversations with potential clients.

**Key Actions:**
- Warm outreach to people you already know in the Omak/Okanogan area
- Post value content on Facebook (using language from your service descriptions)
- Consider a low-friction offer (e.g., $99 network assessment or free 15-min consult)
- Track conversations and follow-ups
