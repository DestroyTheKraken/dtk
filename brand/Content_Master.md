## The Real Problem in Rural Okanogan County

Many homes and businesses rely on Starlink because traditional broadband is slow or unavailable. While Starlink is powerful, most installs are DIY and leave significant performance on the table — especially in mountainous terrain with trees, hills, and variable weather.

Common issues:

- Inconsistent speeds and dropouts in certain rooms or at certain times
- Poor WiFi coverage across the property
- Files and data scattered across Google, OneDrive, iCloud, and devices
- Ongoing monthly subscriptions with no real ownership or control
- No one local to call when something stops working

Most people don't realize how much better their setup can perform with professional optimization — or that they can run their own private cloud at home or on the farm.
