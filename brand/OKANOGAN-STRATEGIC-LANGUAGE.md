# Okanogan County — strategic language for DTK  
**Purpose:** Align Destroy The Kraken messaging with local Comprehensive Plan themes and Economic Alliance strategy — without claiming official county endorsement.

**Sources reviewed (2026-07-22):**
1. **Okanogan County Comprehensive Economic Development & Recovery Strategy (CEDRS)** — Economic Alliance / WA Commerce (public PDF via economic-alliance.com).  
2. **Okanogan County Comprehensive Plan** (adopted; Ordinance 2021-13) — rural character policy language as reflected in county zoning code and public summaries (adopted plan PDF is scan-heavy; themes confirmed via county code / planning materials).  
3. **Okanogan County / CCT Broadband Action Team (BAT)** — countywide broadband vision and feasibility work (WSU Extension / Economic Alliance).  
4. Related: NCWEDD regional context; digital equity themes in NCW broadband reporting.

---

## Themes that match DTK (what the county is already prioritizing)

| Theme (from plans) | CEDRS / plan language (paraphrase + key phrases) | DTK service fit |
|--------------------|--------------------------------------------------|-----------------|
| **Broadband for everyone** | Supporting opportunity: *“Broadband internet – with a goal of full coverage (as close to 100% as possible)”*; SWOT weakness: *inadequate broadband* | Rural Link, check-ins, mesh + Starlink reliability |
| **Resilience** | *Economic resilience* and recovery from shocks (wildfire, pandemic); broadband called out as a **key initiative for long-term resiliency** | Private Hub (own your files), Quiet Watch, offline-capable private systems |
| **Tech-readiness of the workforce** | Education/SWOT: risk of *displacement of lower skill workforce in favor of tech-readiness* | AIDE Desk, CLI/TUI skills, structured prompting — **help residents keep up without leaving the valley** |
| **Small business focus** | Core priority: *business & industry development – with small business as a primary focus* | Packages sized for homes, rentals, small operators — not enterprise hype |
| **Tourism & visitor economy** | Tourism market recovery; Puget Sound + Canadian visitors; natural resource + tourism employment mix | Reliable guest Wi‑Fi, private systems for STRs/realty/tourism operators |
| **Natural resource / outdoor identity** | Agriculture, forestry, tourism-recreation; largest county geography; mountain terrain challenges | “Preserve the open country / working landscape” while modernizing connectivity |
| **Partnership culture** | Vision: *citizens and communities of Okanogan County working together to bring prosperity to all*; mission: partnerships with private, government, tribal entities | Local operator, not fly-by-night; works with what residents already use (Starlink, mesh) |
| **Rural character (Comp Plan / zoning)** | Zoning purpose: provide rural options *while retaining rural character*; public messaging: *preserve the county’s rural character* | Tech that **doesn’t suburbanize the place** — private, small-scale, on **your** hardware |

## Strategic phrasing (approved for marketing)

Use language that **echoes** plan priorities. Do **not** say “endorsed by Okanogan County” unless you have a formal partnership.

### Core positioning line
> Help Okanogan County residents and small operators **stay connected** to changing technology — while **preserving the working rural character** of this place.

### Expanded (hero / about)
> I help local residents stay connected with changing technologies while preserving Okanogan’s open, working-country character — the practical “wild west” of independence, open space, and neighbor-scale business. Reliable connectivity, private systems you own, and clear skills so the future doesn’t force anyone out.

### Aligns with CEDRS without jargon
| Instead of | Prefer |
|------------|--------|
| “Digital transformation” | Stay connected / keep up with tools that matter |
| “Smart county” | Practical broadband and private systems that work here |
| “Enterprise cloud” | Private hub on **your** hardware |
| “Disruption” | Resilience when weather, wildfire, or outages hit |
| “AI revolution” | Better tools + verification so you get useful results |

### Pull-quotes you can paraphrase (attribute when formal)

**Economic Alliance vision (CEDRS):**  
“Citizens and communities of Okanogan County working together to bring prosperity to all.”

**CEDRS supporting priority:**  
Broadband internet — goal of coverage as close to 100% as possible.

**CEDRS SWOT / resilience:**  
Inadequate broadband as infrastructure gap; internet/broadband as key to long-term resiliency; tech-readiness of the workforce as a long-term risk if skills lag.

**Rural character (planning/zoning theme):**  
Retain / preserve rural character while allowing rural uses (farming, low-density living, rural activities).

## How DTK packages map to plan priorities

| Package | Plan alignment |
|---------|----------------|
| **Rural Link** | Broadband reliability, infrastructure capacity, tourism/STR guest Wi‑Fi |
| **Private Hub** | Household resilience + ownership: private cloud, IoT, home theater/audio on client gear (A/V + IoT competency) |
| **Quiet Watch** | Ongoing capacity — light, local support (not a distant call center) |
| **AIDE** | Tech-readiness / skills; prosperity for people who want modern tools without leaving rural identity |
| **Kraken Unstick** | Day-to-day ability for residents/businesses to function |
