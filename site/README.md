## Hello

I’m **Joshua Hickman**. I help people with PCs, Wi‑Fi, and small networks that need to work in real rural life.

If something feels “too complicated to fix,” you’re the kind of person I like to help. We talk first, agree on a simple plan, and work on **gear you own** whenever we can.

---

## Why “Destroy the Kraken”?

The name is a studio brand, not a crypto company or sports team.

For me, the “kraken” is the story that a problem is impossible — dead Wi‑Fi in the shop, files scattered everywhere, or a far-away call center that has never seen your property. **Destroy it anyway** means calm, practical work and clear notes.

You hire **Joshua Hickman**. Independent. Unaffiliated with any other “Kraken” brand.

---

## What I help with

- **PC unstick** — won’t boot, updates, accounts, printers, “something’s wrong”
- **Wi‑Fi & network** — dead spots, mesh, guest networks, Starlink quirks
- **Files & backups** — where stuff lives, simple habits, private options on *your* PC or NAS
- **Scoped private setups** — smaller private networks and careful AI-tool setup, appointment only

**How we start**

1. You send a short message (name, phone, what’s going on)  
2. I call you back — no hard sell  
3. We schedule a local or remote appointment
