Package A · Primary premium

Methow Vacation Rental Network & Guest Experience

Who it’s for: Short-term rental owners, property managers, small lodges, and second-home owners in the Methow.

Outcome: Guests stay connected. Your private network and privacy stay protected.

What we typically cover (always scoped after conversation or site survey):

  • Separate owner and guest Wi‑Fi with strong isolation
  • Starlink and/or local fiber optimization, plus mesh designed for cabin and mountain layouts
  • IoT readiness on the private side (locks, thermostats, lights, basic sensors)
  • Simple owner hand-off notes and guest cards
  • Optional light uptime monitoring

Illustrative project range: $2,400–$4,800 one-time, plus optional low monthly monitoring. Final price follows a real conversation and scope — not a fixed menu item.
