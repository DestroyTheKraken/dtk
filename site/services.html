Package A · Primary recommended

Methow Vacation Rental Network & Guest Experience

Who it’s for: Short-term rental owners, property managers, small lodges, and second-home owners in the Methow who need reliable guest Wi‑Fi without giving guests the owner’s private network.

Outcome: Guests stay connected. You keep control and privacy.

Scope is field-set after a site survey. Typical work includes:

  • Dual / multi-SSID design with strong guest isolation (owner private + guest)
  • Starlink and/or local fiber optimization plus whole-property mesh for cabin and mountain layouts, including seasonal occupancy
  • IoT readiness on the private side (smart locks, thermostats, lighting, basic sensors)
  • Owner hand-off: simple notes, guest SSID/password cards, and an optional remote check path (Tailscale or equivalent) that does not expose the guest network
  • Optional add-on: lightweight uptime monitoring with owner alerts

Positioning: premium, outcome-focused — not a basic “install a mesh kit and leave.” Illustrative one-time range: $2,400–$4,800 (property size and complexity), plus optional low monthly monitoring. Not a fixed menu price; we scope after we talk.

Package B · Complementary or alternative

Sovereign Property Edge & Monitoring

Who it’s for: Remote-property owners, higher-end residences, and privacy-conscious clients who want local security cameras and network health without heavy cloud dependence.

Outcome: Local control of cameras and network health — with less “everything lives in someone else’s cloud.”

Typical scope (after conversation):

  • Private network foundation or upgrade with proper segmentation
  • Local or hybrid NVR / camera integration that prioritizes ownership and minimal cloud exposure
  • Network monitoring (uptime, basic bandwidth/health alerts) on owner-controlled channels
  • Optional local media layer (Jellyfin-style or simple media server) for second homes

Illustrative range: $2,800–$5,500+ depending on camera count and complexity. Sold standalone or as a companion / upsell next to the Vacation Rental package. Scoped after conversation — no black-box mystery stack.
